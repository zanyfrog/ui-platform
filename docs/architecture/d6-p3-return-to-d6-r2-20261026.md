1. Accept the narrow P3 implementation and decide the exact first supported target semantics. The existing standard template is composed and is explicitly outside P3. 7. Define actual owner/session/access-policy continuity, revocation, visibility, expiration and invalidation before production use; authorize before acquisition and reauthorize any later disclosure. Reject superseded generation/late completion. Nonproduction fixtures verify mechanics only.
8. Admit exact actual owner imports/initialization and scoped serial zero-retry nonprotected fixtures for the combined source-bound capture. Retain raw failures and compare current legacy source/history/autosave behavior. A separately approved named operational acquisition must specify real roots/identities, participating writers, private namespace access, exact permissions, acquisition/lease bounds and redacted evidence. No recovery/repair follows a refusal.

Immutable delivery and native activation remain independent later work. Cleanup is disabled until an approved retention/GC contract exists. Complete WP5-F, D6 and WP5-I remain open and unaccepted; all 40 umbrella requirements remain unchecked.
