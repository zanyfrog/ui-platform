## Actual blocker after P2/P3 acceptance

The accepted package/presentation owners now provide immutable content and generation-checked sealed references. They still do not establish one required capture fact: **the real Application's effective package activation is authoritatively bound to the exact selected P2 sealed publication under a currentness/writer fence**.

`AppPackageDeclaration` stores enabled/version/resolution/timestamps, not a seal identity or activation generation. `getActiveAppPackages` resolves mutable catalogs/live package locations and may invoke manifest initialization/catalog refresh. `enableAppPackage` / `disableAppPackage` → `setAppPackageDeclaration` do not join the inspected capture graph on their existing call paths. P2 deliberately preserves those workflows and explicitly leaves their sealed activation integration deferred. A verified selected P2 publication may remain present while the real package is disabled, resolves elsewhere or changes under a nonparticipating writer. Its lease cannot certify otherwise.

P3's selected seal also remains separate from ordinary `activeVersion`. Its sealed manifest/version is historical completed-source evidence, not a fresh applicability check against the real saved source. This needs an owner crosscheck; it cannot be inferred from the request or a valid lease.

Additionally, P2/P3 public readers each acquire their own graph, while Foundation rejects nested graph acquisition. A composable owner read-under-existing-admission helper is needed to satisfy one graph across capture/revalidation. This is a bounded ordinary-source integration gap, not a new authority requirement. Fixing it alone would leave the activation binding absent, so no runtime helper is added merely to manufacture partial progress or a false successful capture.

Exact source references, hashes and retained ordinary-source excerpts are in `evidence/d6-r2-resumption-20261026/`. The integration map and capture/refusal obligations are separate deliverables. No current activation, protected source, registry or policy data was inspected.
